Before you ask

Most requests fall into one of these. The answers link to the right page.

Is a bot down?

Live heartbeat for every bot is published on the status page. If a bot shows degraded or outage, an incident is already being tracked.

What data do you store?

Each bot has its own privacy policy that lists exactly what it stores, why, and how to remove it. Nothing is hidden behind vague language.

How do I delete my data?

Server admins can delete most data directly from the bot's own menus. For a full wipe, email us. Requests are actioned within seven days.
