Privacy policies

Common questions

Where is data stored?

On the bot host, in local JSON files. No external database, no cloud service, no third-party system. Transcripts are uploaded to Discord as attachments and are not retained on the host.

How do I request deletion?

Email hadtoberxr@gmail.com or open a thread in the support server. Requests are actioned within seven days. Server admins can also delete most data directly from the bot's own menus.

Is message content stored?

No. Tixal reads messages inside a ticket channel only at the moment the ticket is closed, to build a transcript. Setuper never reads message content at all. Neither bot requests the MessageContent intent.

Do you share data with third parties?

No. Both bots communicate only with the Discord API. No analytics service, no telemetry, no external database, no outbound webhook, no data broker.
